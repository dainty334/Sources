# D-007 Division range and level length

**Decision:**
- **Division range:** divisors 1–10 and quotients 1–10, so every dividend is 100 or less (for example, 56 ÷ 8). Every item divides evenly.
- **Items per level:** 10.
- **Excluded topics:** division with remainders, long division of multi-digit numbers, fractions and decimals.

**Why:** the researcher asked for the range to follow the current DepEd curriculum. [[Department of Education (n.d.) - MATATAG Mathematics Curriculum Guide]] has learners dividing with the 6, 7, 8 and 9 tables by the end of Grade 3. Grade 4 Quarter 2 then builds on those facts with multi-digit division. The 1–10 facts are therefore the recall base for Grade 4 division. Ten items keep each level to one short sitting.
