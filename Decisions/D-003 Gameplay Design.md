# D-003 Gameplay design

**Decision:** Combine both sources. The game keeps the pre-proposal's quick-tap answers, countdown timer, running score and saved best score/time, and adds Chapter 1's 15 levels in three stages: Easy (1–5), Medium (6–10) and Hard (11–15). Harder levels unlock by performance rules.

**Sources:** [[Pre-Proposal - Division Duel (Final)]] (single mechanic with timer) and [[Chapter 1 - Introduction (Unrevised)]] (15 adaptive levels).

**Still open:** the exact unlock rule, timer length, items per level and division range. These are tracked in [[Game Spec]].

**Applies to:** [[Game Spec]], Chapter I Scope and Objectives, and the game code.
