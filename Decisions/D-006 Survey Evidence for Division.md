# D-006 Survey evidence for division

**Question:** What local evidence shows that Grade 4 learners at Baluan Elementary School find division difficult?

**Problem:** [[Survey Tally (Final)]] asks only about reading comprehension. None of its items covers division. The researcher recalls that most respondents said learners find division hard, but no tally for that exists in the vault.

**Options:**
- Supply a separate division survey tally with its date, respondents and counts.
- Use a dated classroom observation or a teacher statement instead.
- Run a short new survey on division before submission.

**Until settled:** Problem 1 in [[Chapter 1 - Introduction (Revised Draft)]] has national evidence only, and carries a placeholder for local evidence.
