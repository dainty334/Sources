# D-005 Manuscript format

**Decision:** Word exports of the manuscript use:
- US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) paper
- 1.5 in left margin; 1 in top, right and bottom
- Times New Roman 12 pt, double-spaced, justified, 0.5 in first-line indent
- Bold centered chapter titles; bold section headings; bold italic subsections
- APA 7 references with a 0.5 in hanging indent
- Page numbers centered in the footer

**Applies to:** every file in `Manuscript/` exported to `.docx`.
