# CHAPTER I – INTRODUCTION

This chapter presents the background of the project, along with its objectives, scope, and limitations, and discusses its significance and relevance to the community.

## Project Context

The advancement of technology has brought significant changes to the educational system through the use of mobile applications and other digital learning tools. These technologies give learners more opportunities to access educational materials and improve their academic skills. Mobile applications can also support classroom learning by offering interactive activities that allow students to review lessons and practice their skills even outside the classroom.

Today, mobile phones and other digital devices are widely used for communication, entertainment, accessing information, and educational purposes. Educational applications and game-based learning provide students with alternative ways to practice their lessons in an interactive environment. When appropriately developed, educational games can combine learning activities with simple game elements, making practice more engaging and accessible to learners.

Mathematics is an important learning area for elementary students because the skills learned during the early grades serve as a foundation for more complex mathematical concepts. Division is one of the fundamental mathematical operations that students are expected to understand and recall. However, some Grade 4 students may find it challenging to remember basic division facts quickly and correctly, especially when they have limited opportunities to practice through engaging activities.

This study focuses on improving basic division fact recall among Grade 4 students of Baluan Elementary School. The researcher proposes DIVIRUSH: An Adaptive Quick-Tap Educational Game for Enhancing Grade 4 Learner's Division Fact Recall. The proposed application provides students with an interactive method of practicing basic division facts through short and quick-tap activities. It is intended to serve as a supplementary learning tool that can support students alongside their regular Mathematics lessons.

The project addresses several challenges related to learning and practicing basic division facts among Grade 4 students of Baluan Elementary School, which are listed below:

- Grade four students of Baluan Elementary School may have difficulty recalling basic division facts quickly and accurately, which can affect their ability to answer division-related activities.
- Students may lose interest when division facts are practiced repeatedly through conventional exercises without interactive activities.
- Learners may have limited opportunities to practice basic division facts through engaging and interactive activities outside the classroom.
- Traditional printed exercises may provide limited immediate feedback, making it difficult for students to quickly identify whether their answers are correct.
- Students may need additional practice that allows them to repeatedly recall division facts in a simple and engaging way, which DIVIRUSH aims to provide through its quick-tap game-based approach.

## Purpose and Description of the Project

This project explores integrating technology specifically a game-based application to support basic division practice. Observing that traditional teaching methods often lack interactivity and leave students disengaged, the researcher developed DIVIRUSH for Grade Four pupils at Baluan Elementary School. DIVIRUSH is an adaptive quick-tap educational game designed to enhance basic division fact recall. Through quick-tap mechanics, the game provides real-time feedback so learners can instantly check their accuracy. To keep students motivated and appropriately challenged, the application features an adaptive progression spanning 15 total levels split across three stages: Easy (Levels 1–5), Medium (Levels 6–10), and Hard (Levels 11–15). As students advance, the difficulty increases based on built-in performance rules. Intended purely as an interactive supplementary learning tool, DIVIRUSH offers short, repeatable practice activities to strengthen division fact mastery without replacing classroom instruction or formal teacher evaluations.

## Objectives of the Project

The primary objective of the project is to develop an educational game that helps Grade Four students of Baluan Elementary School practice and improve their ability to recall basic division facts through an adaptive and interactive quick-tap experience. The application aims to provide repeated practice, immediate feedback, and an engaging supplementary learning activity that supports the development of division fact recall.

The application has the following qualities:

- **Educational:** The game is developed to provide practice in basic division facts that are appropriate for Grade Four students of Baluan Elementary School.
- **Interactive:** The application uses a quick-tap mechanic that enables students to actively answer division-related questions by selecting their chosen answer.
- **Adaptive:** The application includes adaptive features that adjust to the learner’s performance based on the rules and mechanisms implemented in the game.
- **Feedback:** The game provides immediate feedback after each answer, allowing learners to identify whether their selected response is correct or incorrect.
- **Practice-Oriented:** The application offers short and repeatable division-fact activities that allow Grade Four students to continuously practice and strengthen their targeted skill.
