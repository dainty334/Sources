# Related Projects Comparison

The comparison matrix of existing apps and games for [[Chapter II Draft 2026-11-22]]. Every fact comes from the product's official page or App Store listing, opened on 2026-11-22 (rows 31–39 of [[Chapter II Source Matrix]]). "Not stated" means the opened page did not say. Product descriptions are the developers' own claims, not evidence that a product improves learning. Google Play listings could not be read, so App Store listings and official websites were used.

## Comparative matrix

| Project | Developer | Platform | Target users | Topic | Main activities | Offline? | Difference from DiviRush |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Times Tables Rock Stars | Maths Circle Ltd (UK) | Web browser; iOS/iPadOS app (also listed on Google Play and Amazon Appstore) | Learners aged 6 and up (App Store: 6–16) | Times tables; multiplication and division recall | Quick-fire questions, adaptive games, multiplayer, leaderboards, tournaments, avatars | Not stated | Paid subscription, multiplication-centred, multiplayer; DiviRush is free, offline, division-only and single-player |
| Prodigy Math | Prodigy Education Inc. | Web; iPhone/iPad (iOS 16+) | Grades 1–8 | Standards-aligned mathematics | Fantasy role-playing game: battle monsters by answering adaptive questions; quests, pets, items | No ("online learning") | Online, curriculum-wide game with paid memberships; DiviRush is offline and drills only basic division facts |
| XtraMath | XtraMath (nonprofit, Seattle, USA) | Online program (devices not stated) | Grades K–6 (About page: K–8) | Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division facts | 10-minute daily adaptive practice; placement quiz; progress reports; trophies | No ("online program") | Online drill with few game elements; DiviRush is an offline game with answer bubbles, stars and unlockable levels |
| Math Playground: Dino Park Division | Math Playground LLC | Web browser (desktop, Chromebook, laptop, tablet) | Grades K–6 (game linked to Grades 3–4) | Many topics; this game: "divide numbers" | Short browser games; Dino Park Division has three levels | Not stated (online site) | Broad, ad-supported site of separate games; DiviRush is one structured offline Android game |

## Project details

| Project | Cost (as stated) | Evidence about it (2015–2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Times Tables Rock Stars | App free to download; "requires a low-cost subscription" | None found. A 2024 critique says no formal effectiveness study exists, but only its blog summary was read, so it is not cited. |
| Prodigy Math | Free for teachers and students; paid parent memberships (App Store: Core Monthly $12.99 and others) | Center for Research and Reform in Education (2020): correlational; 577 K–5 students; more use linked to small state-test gains; home use not related; hosted by the developer. |
| XtraMath | Basic program free; paid Pro and school licences | Wells (n.d.), action research run in spring 2015: 36 Grade 4 students; no significant overall difference from flash cards. Not peer reviewed. |
| Dino Park Division | Free, with ads; paid ad-free plans | None found. |
| Operation Math Game | ₱199.00 (Philippine App Store); no ads or in-app purchases | None found. |
| Mathplus | Freely available (design paper) | De Las Peñas et al. (2023): design paper, no learner outcome data. |
