# Related Projects Comparison (v2)

The comparison matrix of existing apps for [[Chapter II Draft 2026-11-22 v2]], cut to three projects at the researcher's request. Times Tables Rock Stars, XtraMath and the Mathplus apps were kept as closest to DiviRush (fact recall that includes division, and a Philippine Android app). Prodigy Math, Dino Park Division and Operation Math Game were removed; they stay recorded in [[Related Projects Comparison]].

Every fact comes from the product's official page, opened on 2026-11-22 (rows 31, 34, 35, 38 and 39 of [[Chapter II Source Matrix]]). "Not stated" means the opened page did not say. Product descriptions are the developers' own claims, not evidence that a product improves learning.

## Comparative matrix

| Project | Developer | Platform | Target users | Topic | Main activities | Offline? | Difference from DiviRush |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Times Tables Rock Stars | Maths Circle Ltd (UK) | Web browser; iOS/iPadOS app (also listed on Google Play and Amazon Appstore) | Learners aged 6 and up (App Store: 6–16) | Times tables; multiplication and division recall | Quick-fire questions, adaptive games, multiplayer, leaderboards, tournaments, avatars | Not stated | Paid subscription, multiplication-centred, multiplayer; DiviRush is free, offline, division-only and single-player |
| XtraMath | XtraMath (nonprofit, Seattle, USA) | Online program (devices not stated) | Grades K–6 (About page: K–8) | Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division facts | 10-minute daily adaptive practice; placement quiz; progress reports; trophies | No ("online program") | Online drill with few game elements; DiviRush is an offline game with answer bubbles, stars and unlockable levels |
| Mathplus Multiplication and Divisibility Games | Mathplus project, Department of Mathematics, Ateneo de Manila University (Philippines) | Android 5.0+ (APK); some on Windows PC | Grades 1–5 (Multiplication Game); Grade 5 (Divisibility Game) | Multiplication tables; divisibility rules; factors | Levelled concept games (skip counting, arrays, repeated addition); click numbers divisible by a divisor; immediate feedback | Not stated (shared through local networks in low-connectivity areas) | Builds multiplication and divisibility concepts without stated time pressure; DiviRush drills division facts for speed with a timer |
| DiviRush | The researcher (BSIT capstone, Ramon Magsaysay Memorial Colleges) | Android 10–14 phones and tablets | Grade 4 learners of Baluan Elementary School | Basic division facts, divisors and quotients 1–10 | Quick-tap answers among 3–4 bubbles with a 15/10/7-second timer; 4 episodes × 3 difficulties × 5 levels; points; 1–3 stars; unlocking | Yes | — |

## Project details

| Project | Cost (as stated) | Evidence about it (2015–2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Times Tables Rock Stars | App free to download; "requires a low-cost subscription" | None found. A 2024 critique says no formal effectiveness study exists, but only its blog summary was read, so it is not cited. |
| XtraMath | Basic program free; paid Pro and school licences | Wells (n.d.), action research run in spring 2015: 36 Grade 4 students; no significant overall difference from flash cards. Not peer reviewed. |
| Mathplus | Freely available (design paper) | De Las Peñas et al. (2023): design paper, no learner outcome data. |
