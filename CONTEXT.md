# DIVIRUSH

The words the game, its code and the manuscript use for how DiviRush is organised. Use these terms exactly; the terms under _Avoid_ mean the same thing and should not appear.

## Language

**Episode**:
A set of division facts grouped by divisor (for example, ÷6, ÷7, ÷8, ÷9), chosen first from the Episode select screen. DiviRush has four Episodes.
_Avoid_: chapter, world, topic, pack

**Difficulty**:
One of Easy, Medium or Hard inside an Episode. It changes how hard the same facts are to answer (number of answer bubbles, how close the wrong answers are, timer length), not which facts appear.
_Avoid_: stage, mode

**Level**:
One playable round inside a Difficulty. Each Difficulty has five Levels.
_Avoid_: round, stage

**Star**:
The level-end rating for correct answers out of 10: one Star for 8, two for 9, three for 10. At least one Star means the Level is passed.
_Avoid_: medal, badge, rating

**Pass**:
Finishing a Level with at least one Star. Passing a Level is what unlocks the next one.
_Avoid_: clear, complete, win

**Trophy**:
One of 26 game rewards for progress, accuracy and practice, earned once and kept, each with its own picture ([[D-023 Trophies and My Records]], [[D-031 More Trophies, Trophy Art and Featured Trophy]]). A Trophy is a game reward, not a measure of the learner's mathematics achievement.
_Avoid_: achievement, badge, medal

**Records**:
The learner's game records: Stars, Levels passed and played, correct answers, best streak and time played ([[D-023 Trophies and My Records]]). Records of play, not grades. Since [[D-030 Profile, Nickname and New Bottom Bar]], a tab on the Trophies screen.
_Avoid_: My Records, stats, scores, report, grades

**Profile**:
The learner's own screen, opened from the pill at the top left of the Main menu: the Nickname, the Buddy, and the counts of Stars, Trophies and Levels passed ([[D-030 Profile, Nickname and New Bottom Bar]]). Kept only on the learner's phone; it is not an account.
_Avoid_: account, user, login

**Nickname**:
The short name a learner types the first time they play, 1 to 12 characters, shown on the Profile ([[D-030 Profile, Nickname and New Bottom Bar]]). A nickname, never the learner's full name.
_Avoid_: username, real name

**Featured Trophy**:
The one earned Trophy a learner picks to show on the Profile and on the Main menu's Profile pill, or None ([[D-031 More Trophies, Trophy Art and Featured Trophy]]).
_Avoid_: badge, showcase

**Buddy**:
The learner's choice of mascot: Ana, Migo, or Both, taking turns ([[D-030 Profile, Nickname and New Bottom Bar]]).
_Avoid_: avatar, character, gender
