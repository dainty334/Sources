# Department of Education (n.d.) – MATATAG Mathematics Curriculum Guide, Grades 1, 4 and 7

**APA 7:** Department of Education. (n.d.). *MATATAG curriculum: Mathematics, Grades 1, 4 and 7*. https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/MATATAG-Mathematics-CG-Grades1-4-and-7.pdf

**What it is:** DepEd's official MATATAG curriculum guide for Mathematics, Grades 1, 4 and 7 (36 pages). It is linked as "Mathematics Grades 1, 4 and 7" from DepEd's page "Curriculum Phase 1 SY 2024-2025" (https://www.deped.gov.ph/matatagcurriculumk147/).

**Date:** neither the PDF nor the DepEd page shows a publication date, so the entry uses "n.d." The DepEd page states that the MATATAG Curriculum was launched on August 10, 2023. If a dated version is found, change the entry to that year.

## Verified findings

- Key Stage 1 standard (p. 16): by the end of Grade 3, learners perform "multiplication and division using 6, 7, 8 and 9 multiplication tables", division of 2- to 4-digit numbers, and estimation of quotients.
- Grade 4 standard (p. 19): Number and Algebra includes "division of up to 4-digit numbers by up to 2-digit numbers, and the MDAS rules".
- Grade 4, Quarter 2, competency 4 (p. 26): divide two numbers with and without regrouping: (a) 3- to 4-digit numbers by 1-digit numbers; (b) 2- to 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers.
- Grade 4, Quarter 2, competency 5 (p. 26): estimate quotients of 3- to 4-digit dividends by 1- to 2-digit divisors using multiples of 10.
- The word "remainder" does not appear anywhere in the guide. Grade 4 Quarter 2 also covers multi-step problems with the four operations and the MDAS rules.
