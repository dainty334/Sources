# Department of Education (2024) – MATATAG Curriculum FAQ

**APA 7:** Department of Education. (2024, July 30). *Frequently asked questions on the policy guidelines on the implementation of the MATATAG Curriculum*. https://deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/FAQs-ON-THE-MATATAG-CURRICULUM.pdf

**What it is:** DepEd's official FAQ on DepEd Order No. 010, s. 2024, issued July 23, 2024. The document says it was updated as of July 30, 2024.

## Verified findings

- DO 010, s. 2024 covers Kindergarten to Grade 10. Grades 4 to 6 form Key Stage 2.
- The MATATAG Curriculum starts in SY 2024–2025 for Kindergarten, Grades 1, 4 and 7, with full implementation in SY 2026–2027.
- Schools may use the Rapid Mathematics Assessment (RMA) among the tools to profile learners.
- Learning materials can be accessed through the DepEd Learning Management System.

## Used for

National situation in Chapter I: Grade 4 is one of the first grades under MATATAG.
