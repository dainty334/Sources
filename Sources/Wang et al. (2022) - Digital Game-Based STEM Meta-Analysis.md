# Wang et al. (2022) – Digital game-based STEM education meta-analysis

**APA 7:** Wang, L.-H., Chen, B., Hwang, G.-J., Guan, J.-Q., & Wang, Y.-Q. (2022). Effects of digital game-based STEM education on students' learning achievement: A meta-analysis. *International Journal of STEM Education, 9*(1), Article 26. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-022-00344-0

**What it is:** A meta-analysis (a study that combines the results of many earlier studies) published online March 17, 2022. Authors and details verified through the Crossref record.

## Verified findings

- 33 studies with 3,894 students, published 2010–2020, in K-12 and higher education.
- Digital games had a moderate overall effect on learning achievement compared with other methods (ES = 0.667, 95% CI [0.520, 0.814]).
- Moderators examined: control treatment, subject, educational level, game type, gaming platform and intervention duration.

**Caution:** The studies span several STEM subjects and levels, so the result does not apply directly to Grade 4 division. The full text was not read; moderator results for primary school and mathematics are not verified.
