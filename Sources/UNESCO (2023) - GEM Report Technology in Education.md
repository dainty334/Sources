# UNESCO (2023) – GEM Report: Technology in Education

**APA 7:** UNESCO. (2023). *Global education monitoring report 2023: Technology in education: A tool on whose terms?* UNESCO. https://doi.org/10.54676/UZQV8501

**What it is:** UNESCO's 2023 Global Education Monitoring Report on technology in education. Open access (CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO). ISBN 978-92-3-100609-8.

## Verified findings

- Technology can support some types of learning in certain contexts.
- The benefits disappear when technology is used in excess or without qualified teachers.
- Much of the evidence on education technology comes from those who sell it, and robust, impartial evidence is scarce.
- Technology should be adopted when it is appropriate, equitable, scalable and sustainable, and should complement face-to-face teaching.

## Used for

Global situation in Chapter I, and the framing of DIVIRUSH as a support resource, not a replacement for teaching.
