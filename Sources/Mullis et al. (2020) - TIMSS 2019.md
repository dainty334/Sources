# Mullis et al. (2020) – TIMSS 2019 International Results

**APA 7:** Mullis, I. V. S., Martin, M. O., Foy, P., Kelly, D. L., & Fishbein, B. (2020). *TIMSS 2019 international results in mathematics and science*. TIMSS & PIRLS International Study Center, Boston College. https://timssandpirls.bc.edu/timss2019/international-results/

**What it is:** The official international report of the Trends in International Mathematics and Science Study 2019. ISBN 978-1-889938-54-7.

## Verified findings

- Philippine Grade 4 learners averaged 297 in mathematics, the lowest of 58 participating countries.
- The Low International Benchmark is 400, so the Philippine average falls below it.
- The Philippines took part only in the Grade 4 assessment in 2019.

National situation in Chapter I: gaps in Grade 4 mathematics.
