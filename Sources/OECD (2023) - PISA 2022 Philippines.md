# OECD (2023) – PISA 2022 Country Note: Philippines

**APA 7:** OECD. (2023). *PISA 2022 results (Volume I and II) – Country notes: Philippines*. OECD Publishing. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i-and-ii-country-notes_ed6fbcc5-en/philippines_a0882a2d-en.html

**What it is:** The OECD country note on the Philippines' results in the Programme for International Student Assessment 2022 (15-year-old students).

## Verified findings

- 16% of Filipino students reached at least Level 2 in mathematics; the OECD average was 69%.
- Almost no Filipino students were top performers (Level 5 or 6) in mathematics.
- Average 2022 results were about the same as in 2018.

**Not verified here:** the mean score of 355 appears in news reports, but was not checked against the OECD table. Do not cite it from this note.
